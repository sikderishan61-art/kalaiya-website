Who's Behind Kallaiya

Meet Md. Masnur Islam Ishan — Founder, CEO & Software Engineer

Md. Masnur Islam Ishan Founder, CEO & Software Engineer, Kallaiya

"Kallaiya exists so clients get one accountable team — not a chain of subcontractors."

Md. Masnur Islam Ishan is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Kallaiya, and a software engineer by profession. He founded Kallaiya to bring SEO, web development, Meta ads, email marketing, video editing and ad creative under one roof, with every campaign planned and run in‑house rather than outsourced.

That structure keeps strategy, creative and execution aligned — the same people who build your ad are the ones watching its performance and adjusting it.

As a software engineer, Ishan designs, codes and ships the whole product himself, from the interface and animation to the back end and search visibility. That hands-on engineering is what makes Kallaiya's websites fast and its campaigns measurable.

Outside the agency he built MCQBook (mcqbook.online), a practice platform that helps SSC students revise chapter by chapter. Building a product for thousands of learners keeps his work focused on speed, clarity and real usefulness.

Packages start at $150, built for small businesses and creators who want a real, managed marketing engine without a large agency retainer.
